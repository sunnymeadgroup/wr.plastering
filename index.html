What I do

From patching a hole to plastering a whole house. If it's plaster, I do it.

Skimming and re-plastering

Walls and ceilings skimmed smooth over old plaster, Artex or fresh board. Ready to paint.

Plasterboarding

Boarding new walls and ceilings, dot and dab, stud work, overboarding cracked ceilings.

Rendering

Traditional sand and cement render and modern render systems. External walls, garden walls and repairs.

Repairs and patching

Holes from rewires and plumbing, water damage, cracks and blown plaster made good and blended in.

Small joinery jobs

Stud walls, boxing in pipes, door linings, skirting and architrave. The bits that come up mid job, sorted without another trade.

Extensions and refurbs

Plastering out new extensions, loft conversions and full house refurbishments for homeowners and builders.
